Open a typical modern note-taking workspace to jot down a three-line idea, and look at what greets you before you even type a single character:

- An AI sparkle prompt offering to "brainstorm, rewrite, or summarize" a blank page
- A sidebar cluttered with template galleries, team spaces, and upgrade badges
- Notification bells, presence avatars, and an oscillating sync spinner in the top corner
- A subscription gate reminding you how many blocks or devices remain on your plan

Every one of those widgets competes for the exact cognitive bandwidth you opened the app to protect. Instead of entering a quiet room to think, you walk into a noisy lobby filled with billboards.

## The Cognitive Tax of Attention-Seeking Tools

Software inherits the incentives of its business model. When a notes product is built to drive daily active engagement metrics, collaborative seats, or AI credit consumption, <mark>calmness becomes a liability</mark>. Interfaces expand until every square centimeter invites another click.

The result is subtle but measurable mental friction. Before your thought stabilizes, your eye flicks toward a status indicator, your attention splinters on an unread counter, or a floating slash-menu suggestion interrupts the rhythm of a sentence. Over weeks of work, people stop trusting their note apps for deep thinking and retreat back to empty `.txt` files or plain sheets of paper.

> Craft in software is mostly subtraction: removing everything that asks for attention so only your own words remain.

Fold exists to prove that web software can feel as composed and respectful as fine physical stationery.

## Tactile Surfaces: The Paper & Ink Design System

Digital minimalism does not mean sterile white rectangles and default system borders. Cold `#FFFFFF` backgrounds paired with `#000000` text create harsh luminance contrast that fatigues the eyes during long evening sessions, while pure `#000000` dark modes feel flat and lifeless.

Fold balances warmth and structural precision across two deliberate themes—**Paper** and **Ink**:

- **Paper (`#f4f0e6` canvas / `#fffdf8` sheet)**: Inspired by warm laid cotton stationery, gently softening glare while keeping body ink (`#191712`) razor sharp.
- **Ink (`#141511` canvas / `#1b1c17` sheet)**: A deep warm-charcoal surface with ivory text (`#f2eee3`) tuned for late-night writing without harsh blue-white haloing.
- **Vermilion Accent (`#b8452c` / `#e07b56`)**: Used sparingly—like a red fountain-pen margin mark—only for active focus, checkboxes, highlights, and primary triggers.

### Concentric Geometry & Specular Catchlights

Physical instruments feel trustworthy because their geometry obeys physical rules. Fold applies a **concentric double-border law** across its floating navbar, workspace frames, and cards:

```css
/* Concentric Radius Law: R_inner = R_outer - Padding */
.frame-outer {
  border-radius: 16px;
  padding: 3px;
  border: 1px solid var(--border);
}

.frame-inner {
  border-radius: 13px;
  border: 1px solid var(--border);
}
```

Paired with a subtle 1-pixel directional specular catchlight along the top rim (`--specular-line`) and micro-scale film grain, components sit with quiet physical weight—never flashy, never distracting, simply solid under your cursor.

### Typography Built for Reading and Thinking

Each typographic voice in Fold has one unambiguous role:

1. **Fraunces (`--font-serif`)** brings editorial warmth to note titles and essay headings, giving personal thoughts a sense of permanence.
2. **Instrument Sans (`--font-ui`)** delivers clean, unhurried legibility for sustained paragraph writing and structured prose.
3. **JetBrains Mono (`--font-mono`)** handles keyboard shortcuts, timestamps, tags, and code snippets with tabular alignment.

## Muscle-Memory Ergonomics & Zen Mode

Visual restraint is only half of digital minimalism; **interaction restraint** completes it. Reaching for the mouse to switch folders, toggle formatting, or search past thoughts breaks flow.

Fold maps core actions to fast, predictable keyboard shortcuts and pairs interactive toggles with subtle, synthesized Web Audio mechanical haptics (`AudioContext` sine/triangle micro-clicks) that you can mute anytime:

| Shortcut | Action | Design Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `⌘K` | Command Palette & Quick Find | Jump to any note or action in milliseconds |
| `⌘.` | Zen Full Screen Mode | Collapse sidebar & note list into pure writing space |
| `⌘⇧9` | Task Checklist Toggle | Convert lines into tactile interactive checkboxes |
| `⌘⇧Y` | Highlight Selection | Mark key phrases with warm paper highlight |
| `N` / `⌥⇧N` | New Note | Capture a thought without triggering browser new-window |
| `T` | Theme Toggle | Shift effortlessly between Paper and Ink |

### Entering Zen Focus Mode

When you press `⌘.`, Fold smoothly collapses the folder sidebar, note list, and window chrome—leaving only your title, clean typography, and gentle margins. There is no "focus score" timer ticking down in the corner, no streak counter judging yesterday's output, and no AI sidebar hovering over your margin.

- [x] Zero feeds, recommendations, or social widgets
- [x] Zero account creation or cookie consent banners
- [x] Zero cloud notifications interrupting deep work
- [x] Full keyboard control + serene Zen Mode (`⌘.`)

When software stops demanding your attention, something remarkable happens: you forget the tool exists, and only the writing remains.
