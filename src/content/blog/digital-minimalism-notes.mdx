Open a typical note-taking app to write down a three-line idea, and look at what greets you before you type a single letter. An AI prompt asks to brainstorm or summarize a blank page. A sidebar shows template galleries, shared spaces, and upgrade badges. A notification bell, presence avatars, and a sync spinner sit in the top corner beside a plan limit banner.

Every widget competes for the attention you opened the app to protect. Fold removes those distractions so the workspace behaves like a quiet desk rather than a crowded dashboard.

## The cognitive tax of attention-seeking tools

Attention-seeking note apps split your focus before a sentence is finished because engagement-driven interfaces reward constant clicking. When software chases daily active usage, collaborative seats, or AI credit consumption, <mark>calmness becomes a structural liability</mark>, pushing writers back toward plain `.txt` files or physical paper.

The friction shows up in small interruptions. Before a thought settles, your eyes drift to a sync indicator, an unread badge, or a floating slash-menu suggestion that breaks the cadence of typing. Over weeks of daily writing, that low-grade visual noise trains you to distrust your workspace for deep thinking.

> Good software design is mostly subtraction: removing everything that asks for attention so only your own words remain.

Fold treats web software like fine physical stationery, keeping the canvas quiet so your own writing carries the weight of the screen.

## Tactile surfaces in the Paper and Ink system

Fold replaces stark `#FFFFFF` and `#000000` web defaults with two warm, calibrated palettes called Paper and Ink. Both themes preserve crisp contrast for long reading sessions while using a restrained vermilion accent exclusively for active focus, checkboxes, highlights, and primary actions.

Cold white backgrounds produce harsh glare during evening work, while pure black dark modes look flat and cause bright text to halo. Fold tunes every surface token around warm paper and charcoal ink:

| Surface Role | Paper Theme Token | Ink Theme Token | Visual Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| App canvas (`--bg-canvas`) | `#f4f0e6` | `#141511` | Warm background margin around the sheet |
| Editor sheet (`--surface`) | `#fffdf8` | `#1b1c17` | Primary reading and writing surface |
| Body text (`--ink`) | `#191712` | `#f2eee3` | High-contrast ink without pure black glare |
| Primary accent (`--accent`) | `#b8452c` | `#e07b56` | Active focus rings, checkboxes, and highlights |

### Concentric geometry and specular catchlights

Physical tools feel predictable because their parts follow consistent geometry. Fold applies a concentric double-border rule across its floating navigation bar, workspace frames, and cards:

```css
/* Concentric Radius Law: R_inner = R_outer - Padding */
.frame-outer {
  border-radius: 16px;
  padding: 3px;
  border: 1px solid var(--border);
}

.frame-inner {
  border-radius: 13px;
  border: 1px solid var(--border);
}
```

Paired with a 1-pixel directional specular line along top rims (`--specular-line`) and subtle film grain, controls sit quietly on the page without heavy drop shadows or animated banners.

### Typography built for reading and thinking

Fold loads only two self-hosted typefaces (`Instrument Sans` and `JetBrains Mono`) so every element on screen has a clear, consistent voice:

1. `Instrument Sans` (`--font-instrument`, mapped to `--font-ui`) handles note titles, headings, body prose, and interface labels with clean, unhurried proportions.
2. `JetBrains Mono` (`--font-jetbrains`, mapped to `--font-mono`) renders keyboard shortcuts, timestamps, tags, storage metrics, and code blocks with tabular alignment.

## Keyboard ergonomics and Zen focus mode

Visual restraint only works when paired with fast keyboard control, because reaching for a trackpad to switch folders or toggle formatting breaks writing momentum. Fold binds every core action to browser-safe shortcuts and adds optional Web Audio micro-clicks (`AudioContext`) that you can mute anytime.

Shortcuts avoid browser-reserved combinations so pressing them never opens a new window or closes your tab by accident:

| Shortcut | Action | Design Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `⌘K` | Command Palette (or Insert Link on selection) | Jump between notes and commands without lifting your hands |
| `⌘.` | Zen Focus Mode | Collapse the sidebar and note list into a full-screen sheet |
| `⌘⇧9` | Task Checklist Toggle | Turn lines into interactive checkboxes |
| `⌘⇧Y` | Highlight Selection | Mark phrases with a warm highlight span |
| `N` or `⌥⇧N` | New Note | Start a fresh note without colliding with browser `⌘N` |
| `T` | Theme Toggle | Switch between Paper and Ink themes immediately |

### Entering Zen focus mode

Pressing `⌘.` collapses the folder sidebar, note list, and surrounding window chrome, leaving only your note title, clean margins, and prose (`Esc` or `⌘.` exits). There is no focus score counting down in the corner, no streak counter judging yesterday's word count, and no AI sidebar hovering beside your cursor.

- [x] Zero feeds, recommendations, or social widgets
- [x] Zero account walls or cookie consent banners
- [x] Zero cloud notifications interrupting deep work
- [x] Full keyboard navigation with Zen Focus Mode (`⌘.`)

When software stops asking for attention, the interface fades into the background and only the writing remains.
